01 / About Cross World

Building across boundaries.

The Cross World Organization is an emerging holding and investment organization focused on developing businesses and exploring opportunities across a diverse range of industries.

Our approach is grounded in long-term thinking: supporting distinct ventures, pursuing ideas with potential, and building a portfolio that can evolve over time. Each business has its own focus while contributing to a broader entrepreneurial vision.

03 / Our Vision

Built for the long term.

We believe meaningful businesses can emerge from very different industries. Cross World's long-term ambition is to develop a diversified group of ventures while remaining thoughtful about where to invest time, capital, and attention.

Our focus is on steady progress, adaptability, and the potential to create lasting value—not growth for its own sake.
